🚀 AURC Atlas Rocket — Structures & Composite Manufacturing

Structures team project for a high-performance competition rocket. My work focused on nose cone design/manufacturing, technical drawings, composite processes, and structural integration.

My contribution

  • Worked as part of the structures team across the rocket build.
  • Focused heavily on nose cone design, manufacturing and finishing.
  • Produced and interpreted CAD/technical drawings.
  • Contributed to composite layup, vacuum bagging, sanding, finishing and integration.

Engineering highlights

  • Improved surface finish by addressing pitting caused by trapped air in chopped-fibre moulding.
  • Used fabric outer plies to improve mould conformity and finish quality.
  • Worked with carbon fibre, fibreglass, aluminium components and structural assemblies.
