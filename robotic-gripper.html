🤖 Robotic Egg Gripper — Design, Simulation & Control

Two-finger robotic gripper designed to handle fragile objects using position and force control. My role was mainly on the design team, including mechanical design and simulation.

My contribution

  • Worked mainly on design and simulation of the gripper system.
  • Contributed to mechanism design using a lead screw and curvilinear finger motion.
  • Supported control and integration work including PID tuning and motor behaviour refinement.

System design

  • Lead screw converts motor rotation into linear and curvilinear finger motion.
  • Force sensor and IR sensor support feedback and object detection.
  • Designed for quick assembly, sensor access and manufacturability.
