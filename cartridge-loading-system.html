🏭 Cartridge Loading System — Internship Mechanical Design

Designed a cartridge loading/sealing mechanism from scratch during internship, including system architecture, CAD assembly, and engineering justification using a weighted decision matrix.

My contribution

  • Designed the system from scratch as an internship project.
  • Created the CAD assembly and physical prototype direction.
  • Compared vertical direct drive and horizontal bevel-gear architectures.
  • Evaluated torque efficiency, height limits, racking resistance, manufacturability, cycle time and reliability.

Engineering highlights

  • Used bevel gears and lead screws to translate motor output into vertical sealing motion.
  • Balanced compact packaging with torque delivery and platform stability.
  • Created a justification report to support architecture selection.
